About the Frank Shields Group

Specialist shielding companies in India and the Middle East since 2006, under one brand.

Our story

From the UAE to India

Frank Shields started in January 2006 in the United Arab Emirates. It was the first local company in the Middle East able to design and manufacture shielding solutions in the region. Since then we have completed more than 150 shielding projects across the Middle East and Africa.

Frank Shields Pvt Ltd was set up in India as the group's manufacturing base. Our first Indian project, in 2019, was the intra-operative MRI suite at Apollo Hospitals, Hyderabad. We then built our own factory in Hyderabad, and today we supply RF cages for Siemens MRI installations across 24 Indian states.

This site covers our medical MRI business in India. Industrial and defence shielding is covered on frankshield.com.

Manufacturing

Two factories in Hyderabad

Our two factories sit side by side, under 30 km from Hyderabad airport. One is a clean facility for electronics and assembly. The other handles metal fabrication.

Factory 1: electrical and assembly

  • MRI monitor manufacturing
  • RF test room for MRI-compatible products
  • MRI-compatible electrical panels and lights
  • RF window and RF door assembly
  • Packing, storage and barcode tracking

Factory 2: panel fabrication

  • CNC laser cutting
  • CNC bending for accuracy and repeatability
  • TIG and laser welding
  • Quality inspection and painting
  • Test assembly, labelling and packing

ISO 9001:2015

Certified quality management, with barcode traceability and QA checks at every stage of manufacture until dispatch.

Logistics across India

The Sanath Nagar ICD terminal is 30 km from our factory, with road and rail links across India.

Export ready

Customs clearance is handled at the ICD in one place, and containers go by rail to Mumbai port for shipment worldwide.
