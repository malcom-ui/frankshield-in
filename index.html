What we do

Everything inside the MRI room, from one manufacturer

From the shielded enclosure to the lighting, displays and non-magnetic equipment, our products are designed to work beside the magnet without creating image artefacts.

MRI RF shielding

Modular galvanised steel, aluminium and copper RF cages for 1.5 T and 3 T scanners, with RF doors, windows, waveguides and filters.

Magnetic shielding

Passive shielding with M36 or 35JN360 silicon steel to contain the 5-gauss line and fit the scanner into tighter sites.

MRI-compatible products

Why Frank Shields

Made in Hyderabad, tested on site

Every panel is laser-cut, CNC-bent, welded and test-assembled in our own Hyderabad factory before it ships. Each cage is numbered for installation, so it goes up quickly on site.

  • RF attenuation of 100 dB from 100 to 128 MHz, plane wave
  • Tested on the last day of installation to IEEE Std 299, with a certificate of conformity
  • Typical installation in 10 to 15 days, depending on room size
  • 5-year warranty on the RF cage system
  • Barcode traceability and QA checks at every stage until dispatch
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management
About our factory
Landmark project

Intra-operative MRI suite at Apollo Hospitals, Hyderabad

Our first project in India was an intra-operative MRI shielding solution for Apollo Hospitals, Hyderabad. It was completed within the agreed six months and has run without breakdowns since 2019.

The suite uses Frank Shields RF doors with soft, non-carcinogenic RF gaskets approved for medical facilities, giving 45 dB acoustic attenuation.
