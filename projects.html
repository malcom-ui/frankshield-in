Featured · 2019

Intra-operative MRI, Apollo Hospitals, Hyderabad

Frank Shields developed and installed an intra-operative MRI shielding solution for Apollo Hospitals in Hyderabad. The project was finished within the agreed six months and has run without breakdowns since 2019.

  • RF swing doors with soft, non-carcinogenic RF gaskets
  • 45 dB acoustic attenuation
  • Manual open, close and latching system
