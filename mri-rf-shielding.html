MRI RF shielding

Modular RF cages that keep outside radio-frequency noise away from the MRI signal, and keep scanner emissions inside the room.

Overview

What an MRI RF cage does

An MRI RF cage is an enclosure built from conductive material. It does two jobs. It blocks outside electromagnetic radiation that would distort the MRI signal, and it stops the radio-frequency energy produced by the scanner from interfering with medical devices nearby.

Frank Shields designs each cage to the scanner manufacturer's siting requirements. We supply the panels, doors, windows, waveguides and filters as one tested system.

  • Designed for Siemens, Philips, GE and other 1.5 T and 3 T scanners
  • Standard room heights of 2.5, 2.8, 3.0 and 3.3 m, or custom
  • Panels pre-assembled at our factory so site work goes smoothly
Shielding systems

Choose the right RF cage for your site

Modular galvanised steel (GI) RF cage

1.5 mm galvanised steel panels for floor, walls and ceiling, bolted with stainless steel contact mesh. Our standard system for Siemens 1.5 T and 3 T scanners.

Modular aluminium RF cage

Lightweight aluminium panels, factory-fabricated and test-assembled. Suited to upper floors and sites where floor loading matters.

Double-layer GI or copper-GI on wooden panels

Two shielding layers give 15 to 20 dB of acoustic shielding and some low-level magnetic shielding. Panels can be trimmed on site for awkward rooms.

Copper RF cage

Custom copper shield rooms for projects that specify copper. Doors and windows are matched to the same RF performance.

FAQ

Common questions

Why does an MRI scanner need RF shielding?
The shield blocks outside electromagnetic radiation that would distort the MRI signal. It also stops the scanner's own RF energy from interfering with medical devices nearby.
How long does installation take?
A typical Frank Shields MRI RF cage takes 10 to 15 days to install, depending on room size and design.
How thick is the shielding?
We use galvanised steel or aluminium from 1.5 mm to 3 mm, depending on the RF specification. Our standard Siemens cage uses 1.5 mm galvanised steel panels.
How is electrical power brought into the room?
We supply a power line filter for each circuit entering the enclosure. The filters are bolted to the outside of the cage, with connections on both sides for wiring.
Does the cage meet scanner manufacturer specifications?
Yes. Our cages are designed to meet or exceed the RF attenuation the scanner manufacturer specifies, and every room is tested on site before handover.
Do you also handle magnetic shielding?
Yes. See magnetic shielding. We supply and install silicon steel shielding to contain the 5-gauss line.
