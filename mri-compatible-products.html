MRI-compatible products

Products that work safely beside the magnet, designed to calm patients and keep the MRI suite running smoothly.

The MRI suite

A calmer patient is a completed scan

Many patients feel anxious when they first see the scanner, and the narrow bore can bring on claustrophobia. Cancelled or abandoned scans cost imaging centres significant revenue over a year.

Our MRI suite products turn a clinical room into a calming space. They are built with non-magnetic housings and Frank Shields driver circuits that create no RF interference and no image artefacts.

Suite ambience

Lighting, imagery and entertainment

Sky-ceiling & wall picture panels

Back-lit nature imagery for the ceiling or wall, in sizes from 600 x 600 mm up to 1200 x 2400 mm. Nature scenes help lower stress and make small rooms feel open.

RGB mood lights

30 m of non-ferrous RGB LED strip in aluminium housings, with a remote to change colour and intensity. Can follow a video signal. Controller and RF filters sit outside the room.

LED down lights & spot lights

Dimmable lights with non-magnetic casings and constant-current drivers. Standard kits: 8 x 16 W down lights or 12 x 3 W spot lights, with DC supply and filters.

MRI-compatible display monitor

43 or 32 inch screens in an RF-tight, non-magnetic stainless steel casing. Wall mount or MRI-safe trolley. HDMI in the console room, fibre-optic link into the room.

MRI-compatible projector

Wall or ceiling mounted, at least 2 m from the magnet iso-centre. Projects onto the wall or gantry over a fibre-optic link through the waveguide.

Audio system & patient camera

An MRI-compatible music system for patient comfort, and a patient monitoring camera so staff can watch the patient from the console room.

Intra-operative MRI

Automatic RF sliding doors

For intra-operative MRI suites, we design and build RF and acoustic shielded automatic sliding doors. They are fingerless and use a pneumatic seal built into the door to reach a minimum of 100 dB RF attenuation at 100 MHz plane wave.

Non-magnetic patient equipment

Ferrous objects near an MRI magnet are a serious safety risk. Our non-magnetic equipment is made for use inside the scan room.

Non-magnetic wheelchairs

Move patients into the scan room safely, with no ferrous parts to be pulled towards the magnet.

Non-magnetic patient trolleys & stretchers

For transferring patients to and from the scanner table inside the MRI room.

Non-magnetic drip stands

IV poles that can stay beside the patient during the scan.

Non-magnetic step ladder

Safe access for patients and staff in the scan room.

Non-magnetic fire extinguisher

MRI-safe fire protection to keep inside or at the door of the scan room.
